A FEW USEFUL THINGS

Before you
get in touch.

Not sure what to ask? These are good places to start.

Can you convert a van I already own?

Use the enquiry form to tell us the make, model, wheelbase and year of your van. The team can discuss suitability and possible next steps.

Which conversion should I choose?

Compare the Tour +, Highlander Classic and Lowlander pages, then tell us how you plan to travel. We can help you compare layouts and options.

Can I visit to discuss the options?

Please call or email ahead so the team can confirm availability and arrange a suitable time.

What should I include in my enquiry?

If you have one, include your van details, how many people will travel, the features that matter most, and your rough timescale.
